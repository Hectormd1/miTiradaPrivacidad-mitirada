Sign in with Apple — Hide My Email and relay addresses

The Application supports Sign in with Apple. If you choose to sign in with Apple and select "Hide My Email", Apple may provide the Application with a relay email address (an address that typically ends with "@privaterelay.appleid.com"). Apple will forward messages sent to that relay address to your real Apple-associated email without exposing your real email to the Application.

  • The Application treats the relay email as a valid delivery address for notifications and account messages. The relay address is unique per user and per application and can be used to contact you via email.
  • The Application uses the Apple sub identifier (a stable Apple user identifier) as the primary account key. The email claim from Apple may only be provided once (the first time a user authorizes the app on a device). Because of that limitation, the Application does not rely solely on the email claim for account identification or linking.
  • When a relay email is present, the Application sets a flag to indicate that the stored email is an Apple relay address. That flag is used to avoid automatically linking accounts from other providers based only on the relay email.
  • If you prefer, you may add a personal (non-relay) email address in your profile at any time. The Application will clearly label relay addresses in the Profile UI and allow you to change or add a personal email address. Providing a personal email can be used for account recovery and direct contact.
